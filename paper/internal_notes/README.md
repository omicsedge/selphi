# Internal working notes — NOT part of any submission

Nothing in this directory is a manuscript file. It is the project's own record:
unfrozen benchmark numbers with their provenance and status, and internal
strategy notes. Do not attach these files to a submission, and do not cite them.

The submitted document is built by `paper/build_docx.sh` from exactly two files:

    paper/selphi2_paper.md        the manuscript
    paper/supplementary_info.md   the Supplementary Information

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `benchmark_lab_notes.md` | consolidated benchmark lab-notes: every number with its source, current vs superseded values, and a FINAL/PROV status per row. Formerly `paper/supplementary_benchmark_tables.md`, whose name invited exactly the mistake this directory prevents. |
| `reviewer_response_points.md` | anticipated reviewer points and our answers, drawn from the review history of Selphi 1. Internal preparation for a response letter. |
